# Case Studies

## 1. Sign vs symptom (T018)

### Leaflet text

| Source | EN | EL |
|---|---|---|
| Bemrist PL, s.4 | Common: may affect up to 1 in every 10 people - pain in muscles, bones or joints (signs of musculoskeletal pain) | Συχνές: μπορεί να επηρεάσουν μέχρι 1 στους 10 ανθρώπους - πόνος στους μυς, τα οστά ή τις αρθρώσεις (σημεία μυοσκελετικού πόνου) |
| Bemrist PL, s.4 | Stop using Bemrist Breezhaler and get medical help immediately if you have any of the following: Common: may affect up to 1 in every 10 people - difficulty breathing or swallowing, swelling of the tongue, lips, or face, skin rash, itching and hives (signs of allergic reaction). | Σταματήστε να χρησιμοποιείτε Bemrist Breezhaler και λάβετε αμέσως ιατρική βοήθεια αν έχετε οποιοδήποτε από τα ακόλουθα: Συχνές: μπορεί να επηρεάσουν μέχρι 1 στους 10 ανθρώπους - δυσκολία στην αναπνοή ή στην κατάποση, διόγκωση στη γλώσσα, τα χείλη ή το πρόσωπο, δερματικό εξάνθημα, φαγούρα και πομφούς (σημεία αλλεργικής αντίδρασης). |

### Problem

Pain is a symptom (felt by the patient), not a sign (observed by a doctor), yet EN says "signs" and EL follows with "σημεία". The leaflet is addressed to patients, and a patient cannot diagnose themselves. A patient can only experience or observe symptoms.

The same leaflet is not consistent: the EN word "sign" is rendered in three different ways ("σημείο", "σύμπτωμα" and the verb "υποδηλώνουν"), whether or not the thing described can be observed by a doctor:

- "signs of illness" / "συμπτώματα της ασθένειας" (opening text)
- "(signs the medicine is unexpectedly tightening the airways…)" / "(υποδηλώνουν ότι…)" (section 2)
- "signs of allergic reaction" / "σημεία αλλεργικής αντίδρασης" and "sign of candidiasis" / "σημείο καντιντίασης" (section 4)

### Evidence

- IATE 261853 defines "sign" as "sign that is evident to a doctor examining a patient".
- AI review (S4, S5): DeepL gives "συμπτώματα" in both sentences; ChatGPT gives "σημεία" (S4) and "ενδείξεις" (S5).

### Decision

- "symptom" and "σύμπτωμα" should be used in EN and EL. "ένδειξη" is equivalent to "σύμπτωμα" when used in this context.
- Leaflet term in the termbase ("σημείο"): needs expert check.

## 2. Mismatches in the Greek leaflets: hives, tingling, stinging (T015, T022, T024)

### Leaflet text

| Source | EN | EL |
|---|---|---|
| Bemrist PL, s.4 | Stop using Bemrist Breezhaler and get medical help immediately if you have any of the following: Common: may affect up to 1 in every 10 people - difficulty breathing or swallowing, swelling of the tongue, lips, or face, skin rash, itching and hives (signs of allergic reaction). | Σταματήστε να χρησιμοποιείτε Bemrist Breezhaler και λάβετε αμέσως ιατρική βοήθεια αν έχετε οποιοδήποτε από τα ακόλουθα: Συχνές: μπορεί να επηρεάσουν μέχρι 1 στους 10 ανθρώπους - δυσκολία στην αναπνοή ή στην κατάποση, διόγκωση στη γλώσσα, τα χείλη ή το πρόσωπο, δερματικό εξάνθημα, φαγούρα και πομφούς (σημεία αλλεργικής αντίδρασης). |
| M-M-RvaxPro PL, s.4 | Uncommon (may affect up to 1 in 100 vaccinees) - Hives. | Όχι συχνές (μπορεί να επηρεάσουν έως 1 στους 100 εμβολιαζόμενους) - Κνησμός. |
| M-M-RvaxPro PL, s.4 | Not known (Frequency cannot be estimated from the available data)* - Itching; inflammation of the fatty tissue under the skin; … | Μη γνωστής συχνότητας (δεν μπορούν να εκτιμηθούν με βάση τα διαθέσιμα δεδομένα)* - Φαγούρα, φλεγμονή του λιπώδους ιστού κάτω από το δέρμα, … |
| M-M-RvaxPro PL, s.4 | Not known (Frequency cannot be estimated from the available data)* - Burning and/or stinging of short duration at the injection site; blisters and/or hives at the injection site. | Μη γνωστής συχνότητας (δεν μπορούν να εκτιμηθούν με βάση τα διαθέσιμα δεδομένα)* - Αίσθημα καύσου και/ή κνησμός μικρής διάρκειας στη θέση της ένεσης, φουσκάλες και/ή εξανθήματα στη θέση της ένεσης. |
| M-M-RvaxPro PL, s.4 | Not known (Frequency cannot be estimated from the available data)* - An illness consisting of muscle weakness, abnormal sensations, tingling in the arms, legs, and upper body (Guillain-Barré syndrome). | Μη γνωστής συχνότητας (δεν μπορούν να εκτιμηθούν με βάση τα διαθέσιμα δεδομένα)* - Μια ασθένεια που περιλαμβάνει μυϊκή αδυναμία, μη φυσιολογικές αισθήσεις, φαγούρα στα χέρια, πόδια και το άνω μέρος του σώματος (Σύνδρομο Guillain-Barré). |
| M-M-RvaxPro PL, s.4 | Not known (Frequency cannot be estimated from the available data)* - Headache; fainting; nerve disorders which can cause weakness, tingling, or numbness; eye nerve disturbances. | Μη γνωστής συχνότητας (δεν μπορούν να εκτιμηθούν με βάση τα διαθέσιμα δεδομένα)* - Πονοκέφαλο, λιποθυμία, διαταραχές των νεύρων που μπορεί να προκαλέσουν αδυναμία, φαγούρα ή μούδιασμα, διαταραχές του οπτικού νεύρου. |

### Problem

- **hives (T015):** "hives" can be used as an informal term for the condition "urticaria" (using a visible sign of the condition to describe it). Bemrist renders it as "πομφούς"; M-M-RvaxPro renders it as "κνησμός" (itching) in one item and as "εξανθήματα" (rashes) in another.
- **tingling (T022):** "φαγούρα" means itching (everyday word); tingling would be "μυρμήγκιασμα". The same error appears in the next item: "weakness, tingling, or numbness" / "αδυναμία, φαγούρα ή μούδιασμα".
- **stinging (T024):** "κνησμός" means "itching", which is a different concept: "stinging" is a painful prickling sensation in the skin, whereas "itching" is not necessarily painful.

In M-M-RvaxPro, three different EN terms are rendered with Greek words for itching.

In M-M-RvaxPro, "Hives" (Uncommon) is rendered as "Κνησμός", while the separate item "Itching" (Not known) is rendered as "Φαγούρα". As a result, hives does not appear anywhere in the Greek table, and itching appears twice, with two different frequencies (uncommon and not known). The same rendering appears in the pre-filled syringe leaflet.

### Evidence

- "tingling" belongs under "paresthesia" (MeSH D010292); "itching" belongs under "pruritus" (MeSH D011537). Therefore, they are in two different branches and do not refer to the same sensation. "tingling" is an everyday word with no entry in IATE; its meaning is recorded in general dictionaries (Merriam-Webster).
- "hives": definition based on the MeSH definition of "urticaria"; no IATE entry.
- "stinging": definition based on Merriam-Webster; no IATE entry.
- AI review (S4, S6, S7, S8): neither DeepL nor ChatGPT repeated these errors.
- Both M-M-RvaxPro leaflets in the EMA product information (vial and pre-filled syringe), section 4 table.

### Decision

- **hives (T015):** recommended term "κνίδωση" (preferred); "πομφός" (acceptable). Both are defensible, but a specialist should confirm. Leaflet term in the termbase ("πομφός"): needs expert check.
- **tingling (T022):** recommended term "μυρμήγκιασμα". Leaflet term in the termbase ("φαγούρα"): rejected.
- **stinging (T024):** recommended term "επίπονο τσούξιμο". Leaflet term in the termbase ("κνησμός"): rejected.

## 3. Gastric vs peptic ulcer (T006)

### Leaflet text

| Source | EN | EL |
|---|---|---|
| Eliquis PL, s.2 | Do not take Eliquis if you have a disease in an organ of the body that increases the risk of serious bleeding (such as an active or a recent ulcer of your stomach or bowel, recent bleeding in your brain). | Μην πάρετε το Eliquis εάν έχετε κάποια νόσο σε ένα όργανο του σώματος που αυξάνει τον κίνδυνο σοβαρής αιμορραγίας (όπως ενεργό ή πρόσφατο πεπτικό έλκος ή έλκος του εντέρου σας, πρόσφατη εγκεφαλική αιμορραγία). |

### Problem

EN "ulcer of the stomach" is a lay use of the term, instead of "gastric ulcer" as found in IATE 1547325. EL "πεπτικό έλκος" is a broader term that includes "γαστρικό έλκος" (ulcer of the stomach), as well as ulcers of the oesophagus or duodenum. Therefore, it is not accurate.

### Evidence

- "gastric ulcer": IATE 1547325.
- "peptic ulcer": IATE 1659247. Definition of "πεπτικό έλκος": έλκωση του βλεννογόνου του οισοφάγου, του στομάχου ή του δωδεκαδακτύλου, που προκαλείται από τη δράση του όξινου γαστρικού υγρού.
- Related: IATE 1520801 ("ulcer", broader).
- AI review (S3): both tools give "έλκος στο στομάχι", which agrees with termbase EN (same concept).

### Decision

- Recommended term: "γαστρικό έλκος".
- Leaflet term in the termbase ("πεπτικό έλκος"): rejected.

## 4. Gaps in IATE (T002 Factor Xa and others)

### Leaflet text

| Source | EN | EL |
|---|---|---|
| Eliquis PL, s.1 | This medicine helps to prevent blood clots from forming by blocking Factor Xa, which is an important component of blood clotting. | Αυτό το φάρμακο βοηθάει στην πρόληψη του σχηματισμού θρόμβων στο αίμα, εμποδίζοντας τον Παράγοντα Xa ο οποίος είναι ένα σημαντικό συστατικό της πήξης του αίματος. |

### Problem

"Factor Xa" translates directly into Greek as "Παράγοντας Xa", which is also the established medical term. However, IATE has no entry for "Factor Xa". The nearest entry, "Factor X", is a different concept (the inactive form; "Factor Xa" is the activated form) and has no definition.

This is not an isolated case among the 10 terms reviewed in [ai-suggestions-review.md](ai-suggestions-review.md):

- **No IATE entry:** 7 of 10. Most are patient-language symptoms (T015 "hives", T019 "itching", T022 "tingling", T024 "stinging"). A possible explanation is that everyday words are not treated as terminology, so they are not entered in the database. The others are T002 "Factor Xa" and two descriptive phrases (T007 "bleeding in the brain", T023 "eye nerve disturbance").
- **Entry without a Greek definition:** all 3 terms that are in IATE (T005 "stroke", T006 "ulcer of the stomach", T018 "sign") have no Greek definition in the entry; the Greek definition was written from the English one.
- **Entry without a Greek term:** IATE 1542580 "optic nerve" (the closest related entry for T023 "eye nerve disturbance") has an English definition and terms in 8 EU languages and Latin, but no Greek term.

### Evidence

- T002 "Factor Xa": no IATE entry; definition based on NLM MeSH D015951.
- IATE 1542580 "optic nerve": checked on new.iate.europa.eu, 15/11/2026.
- Coverage of the 10 reviewed terms, copied from termbase.xlsx (`iate_id`, `definition_source`):

| ID | Term (EN) | IATE ID | Definition source (termbase) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T002 | Factor Xa | none | own, based on NLM MeSH |
| T005 | stroke | 1289336 | EN IATE 1289336/ EL own, based on the EN definition by IATE |
| T006 | ulcer of the stomach | 1547325 | EN own, based on IATE 1520801 definition used for' ''ulcer''/ EL no IATE definition , own, based on EN 1659247 IATE definition of 'peptic ulcer' |
| T007 | bleeding in the brain | none | own, based on NIH NLM MeSH for 'Cerebral Hemorrhage' and 'Brain Hemorrhage, Traumatic' |
| T015 | hives | none | based on MeSH definition of 'urticaria' |
| T018 | sign | 261853 | IATE 261853 / for EL own, based on 261853 |
| T019 | itching | none | NIH NLM MeSH 'Pruritus' D011537 |
| T022 | tingling | none | own, based on MeSH D010292, Merriam-Webster dictionary |
| T023 | eye nerve disturbance | none | EN own, based on MeSH D009900/ EL own, based on D009900 |
| T024 | stinging | none | own, based on Merriam-Webster dictionary |

### Decision

- Where IATE has no entry or no definition, the definition was written from other cited public sources (NLM MeSH, PubMed Central, Merriam-Webster); the source is recorded in `definition_source`.
- T002 is marked "needs expert check" because no official terminology source confirms the Greek term, and the definition is my own (based on NLM MeSH D015951).
- Recommendations for working with IATE:
  - log in (user preferences and presets);
  - use new.iate.europa.eu and select several source and target languages at once, so a term is found whichever language it is in;
  - save the settings as a preset or bookmark the search permalink;
  - for large-scale work, use Download IATE instead of single searches.
